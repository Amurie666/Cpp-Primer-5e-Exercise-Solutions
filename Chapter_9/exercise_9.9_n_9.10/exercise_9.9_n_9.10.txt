Exercise 9.9:
The begin() function:
	-has an overloaded, const-qualified version that returns a const iterator.
	-returns a non-cost iterator referring to the first element of the container when called on a non-const container.
	-returns a const iterator when called on a const container.
The cbegin() function:
	-always returns a const iterator referring to the first element of the container.
	-can be called on both const and non-const objects, and it thus has an overloaded, const-qualified version.
