(pos.first) fetches the first item of the pair returned by authors.equal_range(search_item), which, in theory, is either an iterator pointing to the first element with key greater than or equal to search_item or off-the-end iterator. Practically, however, (pos.first) will never return off-the-end iterator or an iterator pointing to element with key strictly greater than search_item because the for loop would have not been executed otherwise. ((pos.first)->second) fetches the second item of the pair to which pos.first points.
