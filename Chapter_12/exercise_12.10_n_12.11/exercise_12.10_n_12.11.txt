Exercise_12.11

	Memory leak. The argument passed to process is independent of p and thus has a reference count of 1. 
	When process is done executing, the reference count decreases back to 1, but since the temporary argument is
	destroyed upon executing the next line, the reference count becomes 0 and the object initially pointed
	to by p is freed, invalidating p.
